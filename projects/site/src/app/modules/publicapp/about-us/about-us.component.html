Our Story

Since 2015, we began with project-based work, classroom training, and online live sessions for the oil & gas value chain. What started as a bootstrapped remote team is now a profitable company of 20+ passionate educators serving learners worldwide.

In 2025, we launched our self-learning portal, driven by autonomy, measurable impact, and the belief that high-quality education should be accessible to every engineer, regardless of geography or schedule.
