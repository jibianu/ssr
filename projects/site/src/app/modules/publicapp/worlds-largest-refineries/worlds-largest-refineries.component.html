World's largest refineries

A concise educational overview of major refining complexes worldwide and their scale in global energy supply.

Refining capacity is often measured in barrels per day (bpd). The largest sites combine crude distillation, conversion units, and petrochemical integration—supporting fuels, feedstocks, and regional energy security.
