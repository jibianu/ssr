ⓘ Payout Threshold

If your earnings for a given month are below the minimum payout threshold, they will carry over to the next month and no payout will be made. Once your total unpaid earnings exceed the threshold, you will receive a payout according to your approved payout schedule.

ⓘ Withholding Tax Status

You may need to submit tax documentation once you start earning. When you earn revenue, you may be prompted to submit tax details as required by your country. Contact support if you have questions about tax withholding.
